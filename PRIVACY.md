# BookQuest privacy policy

*Updated August 2026. This is the same text the app shows under Settings →
About → Privacy policy.*

## Your books stay yours

A file you add to the library is copied into the app's private storage on your
device. It is never uploaded anywhere, not even while you are signed in.

## Without an account, nothing leaves the device

The whole app works signed out. In that state everything — progress, points,
flashcards and settings — is kept on the device only.

## What is stored when you sign in

With an account, these are held on Google Firebase servers:

- your name and email address
- the profile picture and bio you wrote
- your school
- your reading progress
- the classes you belong to, and what you hand in to them

That is what lets you carry on from the same place on another phone.

## Who can see it

A teacher sees who joined their class, what was handed in, and the quiz
results — for their own classes only. Students see each other's messages in the
class stream. Nobody else sees anything.

## No adverts, no tracking

There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking of any kind in this app.
Nothing is sold or passed to a third party.

## Deleting

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete your
account. That removes the stored copy and the account itself. The books on your
device remain yours.

## Crash reports

If the app crashes, a report is written to your device and nowhere else. It is
only sent if you choose to copy it and send it.
